About the game

The runner game with two worlds at once

Shadow Runner: Two Worlds is a puzzle runner for Android and iPhone. You control two versions of the same character at the same time: your physical self runs through a bright reality world on the top half of the screen, and your shadow runs through a dark shadow world on the bottom half. Tap to switch which one is active, swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide.

The two worlds play by different rules. Walls stop your physical self, but your shadow phases straight through them. Bright light, on the other hand, destroys your shadow. Collect coins in reality and crystals in the shadow world, and solve cross-world puzzles where a lever above opens a gate below, a shadow plate builds a bridge above and a street lamp floods the shadow world with light.

Play 30 handcrafted levels in six worlds with an interactive tutorial, three-star scoring, checkpoints, lives and hints. Take on the Daily Challenge, which is the same for everyone each day, or unlock Endless mode after World 1. Shadow Runner works fully offline and has no ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no tracking.

Support

Questions? Switch over here.

Answers to the most common questions about Shadow Runner.

How do I control the runner and the shadow?

Tap to switch which form is active. Swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide. Your physical self runs through the bright reality world at the top, your shadow through the dark shadow world at the bottom.

I'm stuck on a level. Any tips?

Use a hint, and look at the other world: a lever above can open a gate below, and a shadow plate can build a bridge above. Walls stop your physical self but your shadow phases through them, and bright light destroys your shadow.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Everything, including the Daily Challenge and Endless mode, works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. Items in the collection are cosmetic only.

Which accessibility options are there?

Left-handed mode, a high-contrast mode, reduced motion, large touch targets, text labels on colour cues and keyboard support.

I lost my progress. Can you restore it?

Progress is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us, so it can't be restored after uninstalling the game or resetting progress.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall it. Nothing is stored anywhere else. See the privacy policy.
